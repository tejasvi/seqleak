How to read the verdict. FAIL marks a leak at the level that manual audits of DNA benchmarks count as a defect, such as copies of test items in the training data, negatives constructed from positives, artefact or motif shortcuts, or position confounds. WARN marks the same signals below that level, or a composition baseline that a model must beat. It is common on tasks without a documented leak, so treat it as a reason to look closer. PASS means that no check reached its warning threshold, and SKIP that the input lacks what the check needs (coordinates, distance fields, an aligner, or items with several sequences). The linter reports only what its checks measure. It checks position only when distance fields are given, copies of other benchmarks only with --reference, membership in a reference genome only with --genome, and has no check for transductive arrangements; see "Known limitations" in the seqleak README.
